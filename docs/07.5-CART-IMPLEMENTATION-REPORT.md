## 2. Objective

Phase 07.5 establishes the production-grade, user-isolated, authenticated Cart Domain for the Nexora Commerce Platform on top of PostgreSQL, Sequelize, server-side sessions, CSRF protection, and the Product catalog domain.

The implementation guarantees:
- Complete tenant/user cart isolation with zero cross-user data leakage (Anti-IDOR).
- Strict integer paise monetary arithmetic (`price_paise`, `line_total_paise`, `subtotal_paise`).
- Product stock availability validation for new and existing cart items without inventory locking.
- Race-safe concurrent `CartItem` mutations within PostgreSQL transactions.
- Zero N+1 queries through bounded eager loading and explicit column projections.
- Information minimization in DTO serialization (no raw internal inventory fields exposed).

## 6. Database Mapping & Associations

### Entity Relationships
- `User (1) ──── (1) Cart` (`user_id` unique FK in `carts`, `onDelete: 'CASCADE'`)
- `Cart (1) ──── (N) CartItem` (`cart_id` FK in `cart_items`, `onDelete: 'CASCADE'`)
- `Product (1) ──── (N) CartItem` (`product_id` FK in `cart_items`, `onDelete: 'RESTRICT'`)

### Frozen Schema Compliance
All database operations utilize migration `004_create_carts_and_cart_items.cjs` created in Phase 07.2 without modifying or re-running schema alterations.
- `carts`: `id` (UUID PK), `user_id` (UUID UNIQUE FK), `created_at`, `updated_at`.
- `cart_items`: `id` (UUID PK), `cart_id` (UUID FK), `product_id` (UUID FK), `quantity` (INT, `1 <= quantity <= 10`), `created_at`, `updated_at`.
- Unique constraint: `uq_cart_items_cart_product` on `(cart_id, product_id)`.

## 8. Cart Invariants & Business Rules

1. **One Cart Per User:** Database unique constraint on `carts.user_id` enforced with concurrent `findOrCreate` race handling.
2. **One CartItem Per (cart_id, product_id):** Composite unique constraint `uq_cart_items_cart_product` ensures duplicate product additions increment line quantity rather than creating separate rows.
3. **Quantity Boundaries (1–10):** Enforced at Zod validation layer, Sequelize model layer, and PostgreSQL check constraint (`chk_cart_items_quantity`). Cumulative additions cannot exceed 10.
4. **Server-Authoritative User Identity:** `userId` is strictly extracted from `req.user.id`. Client-supplied user/cart identifiers in body or query are strictly rejected.
5. **No Inventory Reservation or Stock Deduction:** Cart operations do not lock product rows for checkout, do not modify `reserved_quantity` or `stock_quantity`, and do not create `inventory_reservations` records.

---

## 9. Security Defenses Verified

- **Anti-IDOR (Insecure Direct Object References):** All `CartItem` operations enforce ownership by joining `Cart` on `Cart.user_id = req.user.id`. Cross-user item IDs return sanitized `404 CART_ITEM_NOT_FOUND` without leaking item existence.
- **Double-Submit Cookie CSRF:** Mutating endpoints (`POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`) require matching `nexora_csrf` cookie and `x-csrf-token` header verified with `crypto.timingSafeEqual`.
- **Parameter Tampering / Mass Assignment:** Zod schemas reject extra parameters (`price_paise`, `userId`, `cartId`, `stock_quantity`, `reserved_quantity`) with HTTP 400.
- **SQL & Operator Injection Defenses:** Parameterized Sequelize queries with strict UUID type validation prevent injection attacks.
- **Information Minimization:** `toCartItemDTO` exposes only customer-relevant attributes and `available_quantity`, hiding internal fields `stock_quantity` and `reserved_quantity`.
- **Logging Redaction:** Sensitive session cookies, CSRF tokens, and credentials are excluded from request logs.

---

## 10. Inventory Boundary Verification

Cart operations act purely on the shopping cart state:
- Adding items to cart: **No inventory reservation created.**
- Updating quantities: **No stock deducted.**
- Removing items / Clearing cart: **No reservations released (none existed).**
- Product inventory counters (`stock_quantity`, `reserved_quantity`) remained invariant before and after all cart tests.

---

## 11. Money Handling & Strict Integer Arithmetic

- All prices and monetary calculations use integer INR paise (`price_paise`).
- `line_total_paise = price_paise * quantity`.
- `subtotal_paise = SUM(line_total_paise)`.
- No floating-point division or arithmetic is performed during cart derivation.

---

## 12. Query Efficiency & Bounded Database Access

- Cart retrieval eager loads `CartItem` and active `Product` (`is_deleted: false`) in a bounded query structure with explicit column projections.
- Zero N+1 queries: Fetching a cart with multiple line items does not execute per-item product queries.
